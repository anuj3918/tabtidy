TabTidy – Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2026

TabTidy ("the extension") sorts your open Chrome tabs alphabetically by domain when you click the extension icon.

Data Collection

TabTidy does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The extension reads the URLs of your currently open tabs solely to determine their domain for the purpose of sorting. This information is used in-memory only and is never sent anywhere — not to any server, third party, or remote service of any kind.

Permissions

TabTidy requests the tabs permission. This is used exclusively to read and reorder tab URLs within the browser. No tab content, browsing history, or personally identifiable information is accessed beyond what is necessary to perform the sort.

Third Parties

TabTidy does not use any analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or third-party services.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue at github.com/anuj3918/tabtidy.
